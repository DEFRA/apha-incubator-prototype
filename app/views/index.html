APHA incubator design resource

Design work for an early warning service for exotic animal diseases. It brings our decisions, prototypes and test plans together, so the team can see where we are and why.

! Warning This is work in progress. All data is illustrative and the disease names are fictional. Pages marked "Early ideas" are not designs.

Background

The story so far: we started by preparing to test the risk slice, tried ideas for how to enter risk, then realised we needed to test the wider concept first. We chose the priority view to do that. These earlier documents come from the first stages. Read how we got here for the full path.

About this resource

This site is also the prototype. The design pages above are static snapshots. When the design is refined, the priority view will be rebuilt here as prototype pages using GOV.UK Frontend, ready to test with users.

Where things are kept:

  • app/views holds this home page, the design notes and the documents. Prototype pages will go here too
  • app/assets/design holds the design snapshots: priority view options, wireframes and images
