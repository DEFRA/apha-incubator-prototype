Round 2: priority view iteration

The second round of design and testing, iterating the priority view after round 1.

All data is illustrative: the disease names are real but the ratings are invented. What we learned in round 1 is in the design log and on the round 1 page.

Summary of version 2

Version 2 is the first set of changes after the research debrief, copied from round 1 v1.1, and the version to take into user research. It is a draft until the team has confirmed it. Version 2.1 will follow the team’s feedback.

What we want to learn

  • Whether people read preparedness the right way round with the new wording, and whether a count or a percentage is clearer.
  • Whether the “i” buttons and the levels in order make the labels clear.
  • Whether people can say why the African swine fever risk is very high, and what has changed.
  • What people expect to see under risk, impact and preparedness, and whether a summary or a page for each is needed.
  • Whether the table or the cards work better for the same task.

The full list is on open questions for research.

Versions in this round

Each version is a frozen copy of the prototype in its own folder, so what we tested stays as it was. To change the prototype, copy the latest version folder to a new version (for example v2.1), make the changes in the copy, and add a row here with what changed and why.
