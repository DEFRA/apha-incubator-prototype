Round 1: priority view and risk exploration

The first round of usability testing for the incubator’s 100 days. It has two parts: a design test of the priority view, and an exploration of risk.

This round tests the design. It builds on the earlier user research behind the nine user needs. All data is illustrative and the disease names are fictional. Last updated 12 November 2026.

The two parts

The two parts of round 1
Part What we want to learn Status
Priority view design test Can people tell which diseases need action first, and what would they want from the pages behind it? Does preparedness work better as two measures or one? Prototype v1.1 is ready, in two versions Ready to test
Risk exploration What do people want from risk, and how should they move between diseases and risk? No prototype yet, only early ideas Early ideas

Open the prototype

  • Separate preparedness (control measures and response options): cards view or table view
  • Combined preparedness (one status): cards view or table view

Either view can be the first one, and either preparedness version. Which to start on is for the user researcher and the wider team to decide. Each page links to the other version and keeps the chosen view. Pages that are not part of the round show a short "not part of the test" page.

Versions in this round

Each version is a frozen copy of the prototype in its own folder, so what we tested stays as it was. If we tweak the prototype during the round, we copy the folder to a new version (for example v1.1) and add a row here with what changed and why.

Prototype versions in round 1
Version What it is Status
Priority view v1.1, separate preparedness 12 November 2026. After the team design review: all four measures in the priority score, risk on six levels, impact areas and change display removed. Control measures and response options are separate. What changed Ready to test
