Risk exploration

Early ideas

Part of round 1. We want to learn what people want from risk, before we design the risk page. There is no prototype yet, only early ideas.

These notes are from the design team, to help the user researcher plan the research. They are not a test plan. The method, tasks and participants are for the researcher and the wider team to decide. Last updated 12 November 2026.

On this page

  • Why risk is explored this way
  • What we would like to learn

Why risk is explored this way

The team decided not to test the risk page on its own. People meet risk and impact as part of the priority view, so the priority view is the way in. The same research can also ask what people want from risk, impact, control measures and response options, and we build those pages from what they say.

What we would like to learn

These are the design team’s questions. Use, change or replace them.

  • What do people want to know about the risk of a disease arriving, and what would they decide from it?
  • Which way do people want to enter risk: by disease, by layer (risk, impact, control measures, response options), or both?
  • Do they want to compare one measure across every disease, or look at everything about one disease?
  • What changes in risk do they want to see, and how quickly?
  • Does impact need its own page, or does it sit with risk?

What we have so far

These are very early thinking, not designs.

Prototype versions

None yet. When there is a risk prototype it will be a frozen copy in its own folder, listed on the round 1 page. In the priority view v1.0 prototype, the Risk link in the navigation leads to a "this page is not part of the test" page that shows what the person chose.

Open questions

  • Sections or pages? Is each of risk, impact, control measures and response options a separate page or a section of one page? Does impact need its own page?
  • Disease first or layer first? Which way do people want to enter risk, and does it depend on their role?
  • Do the risk levels line up? The prototype shows risk as five named levels. Do they line up with the five risk levels in the monthly report?
  • How do people reach a risk page? If they miss the navigation, we try column headings as links.
  • Real data. What does the monthly risk report give us, and how often does it change?

Wider questions are in the design log.
