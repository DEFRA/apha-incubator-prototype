What the priority bands mean

  • Act now Score of 15 or more. The main focus for resources.
  • Prioritise Score of 10 to 14. Close the preparedness gaps.
  • Monitor Score of 4 to 9. Review readiness and watch the risk.
  • Maintain Score of 1 to 3. Keep current arrangements.

How the priority score is worked out

Priority is risk of incursion multiplied by impact, giving a score from 1 to 25. A higher score means we should act sooner. Each is rated from 1 to 5.

  • Risk of incursion: how likely the disease is to reach the UK. Rare, unlikely, moderate, likely or almost certain
  • Impact: how much harm it would do if it arrived. Insignificant, minor, significant, major or severe

Control measures and response options show how many items still need review. They sit beside the priority score and do not change it.
