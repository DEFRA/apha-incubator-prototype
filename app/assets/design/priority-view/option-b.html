How the priority score is worked out

Priority is a score out of 5. A higher score means we should act sooner. It brings together four scores, each out of 5.

  • Risk of incursion: how likely the disease is to reach the UK. 5 is the most likely
  • Impact: how much harm it would do if it arrived. 5 is the most harm
  • Control measures: how well we can stop it entering. 5 is excellent
  • Response options: how well we can deal with it if it arrives. 5 is excellent

A well-prepared disease scores lower even when the threat is high. A poorly prepared disease stays near the top even when it is unlikely to arrive.
